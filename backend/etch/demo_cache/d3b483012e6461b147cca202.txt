## Reasoning

**Requirements:** STM32F103C8T6 logger, microSD (SPI), DS3231 RTC with CR2032 backup, ADS1115 on screw terminals, USB-C power, SWD, user LED, 3.3 V system.

**Power tree:** USB-C VBUS → 1206 fuse → 5 V node (TVS USBLC6 clamps VBUS and D±) → AP2112K-3.3 LDO (600 mA rated, EN tied to VIN). Load budget on 3V3: STM32 ≈ 40 mA at 72 MHz, microSD up to ~100 mA write bursts, DS3231 < 1 mA, ADS1115 < 1 mA, LEDs ~4 mA → ~150 mA typical, 300 mA rail budget. 10 uF on LDO in/out, plus 10 uF bulk at the MCU and 100 nF at every VDD/VDDA/peripheral supply pin. Two 5.1 k CC pull-downs advertise a UFP sink.

**MCU & I/O map (STM32F103C8T6):**
- SPI1: PA5 SCK, PA6 MISO, PA7 MOSI, PA4 CS → microSD; PB1 card-detect (internal pull-up in firmware).
- I2C1: PB6 SCL, PB7 SDA shared by DS3231 (0x68) and ADS1115 (0x48, ADDR→GND), 4.7 k pull-ups to 3V3.
- PA0 ← DS3231 INT/SQW (alarm wake), PA1 ← ADS1115 ALERT/RDY.
- PB0 → user LED via 1 k (~2 mA green).
- PA11/PA12 wired to USB D−/D+ with 1.5 k D+ pull-up so the same USB-C port can also serve CDC log download; TVS sits across those lines.
- SWD: PA13/PA14 + GND + 3V3 on a 1×4 header; NRST with 100 nF and a reset button; BOOT0 10 k to GND.
- 8 MHz 3225 crystal, 2× 20 pF; VBAT tied to 3V3 (RTC backup handled by the DS3231 instead).

**Peripherals:** DS3231 VBAT → CR2032 holder (+), 100 nF on VCC. ADS1115 AIN0/AIN1 to a 3-pin screw terminal (AIN0, AIN1, GND) through 1 k series resistors to limit fault current; unused AIN2/AIN3 grounded to avoid floating inputs. microSD VDD gets 100 nF locally and shares the 10 uF bulk cap.

**Trade-offs:** AP2112K chosen over AMS1117 for low dropout and small size; adequate since peak current is well under 600 mA. A microSD 10 uF local bulk and CS pull-up were omitted to stay within the 38-component limit — firmware must configure PA4 high before SD init. Signal input range is 0–3.3 V single-ended (or differential) unless a divider is added.
