## Reasoning

**Requirements → architecture.** A two-motor robot controller with an ESP32-S3 (native USB, no CH340), DRV8833 dual H-bridge, ultrasonic + two servos, powered from a 7–12 V barrel jack. Single 2-layer board, ~38 parts.

**Power tree.** Barrel jack (7–12 V) → SS34 Schottky in series for reverse-polarity protection (~0.4 V drop at 1 A, fine at 12 V in) → `VIN`. TPS54302 buck (3 A, up to 28 V in) makes `5V`: 10 µF in, 22 µF + 100 µF electrolytic out (servo/motor transients), 4.7 µH inductor, 100 nF bootstrap, EN via 100 k to VIN, feedback 100 k / 13.3 k → 0.596 × (1 + 7.52) ≈ 5.08 V. AMS1117-3.3 (SOT-223, dissipates ≈ 0.9 W at 500 mA — heat-sink into copper) makes `3V3` for the ESP32-S3 (≈ 500 mA Wi-Fi peak). USB VBUS is diode-OR'd into `5V` through a second SS34 so the board programs from USB alone; motors/servos should use the jack.

**Motor driver.** DRV8833 max VM is 10.8 V, so a 12 V jack input cannot feed VM directly. VM is therefore on the 5 V rail (suits 3–6 V TT gearmotors); 10 µF + 10 nF VCP cap on VM, 2.2 µF on VINT, sense pins grounded. AIN1/2, BIN1/2 on IO4–IO7 (all PWM-capable), nSLEEP on IO21 for software enable, nFAULT (open-drain) pulled up 10 k to 3V3 on IO15. Motor outputs to two 2-pin screw terminals.

**MCU & I/O map.** ESP32-S3-WROOM-1: EN 10 k pull-up + 100 nF + reset button; IO0 boot button; 10 µF + 100 nF decoupling. USB-C: D+/D- straight to IO20/IO19, USBLC6 TVS on D+/D-/VBUS, separate 5.1 k on CC1 and CC2. IO16 = TRIG; HC-SR04 ECHO is 5 V so it drops through a 1 k/2 k divider to IO17 (3.33 V). Servo signals on IO18 and IO8; servo VCC from 5 V. Avoid IO35–37 (octal PSRAM). Green power LED on 3V3 via 1 k (~1.5 mA).

**Trade-offs.** No inrush fuse (parts budget) — add one if the robot has large motors. Buck 3 A budget: two servos (~1 A peak each) + two motors + ESP32 is tight under stall; note it. Keep the buck's SW node short, and keep the WROOM antenna at the board edge away from the barrel jack.
