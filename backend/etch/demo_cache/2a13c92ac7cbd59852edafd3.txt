## Reasoning

**Requirements:** USB-C powered Wi-Fi sensor node: SHT31 (T/RH), BME280 (pressure), WS2812B status LED, boot/reset buttons, Qwiic expansion, firmware upload over USB. Indoor, mains-adjacent, no battery.

**Architecture:** ESP32-WROOM-32E is the natural fit — plenty of GPIO, one I2C bus shared by SHT31 (0x44), BME280 (0x76) and the Qwiic port, and Wi-Fi built in. Since WROOM has no native USB, a CH340C USB-UART bridge handles programming on UART0 (IO1/IO3).

**Power tree:** USB-C VBUS → 1 A fuse → 5V rail (~800 mA budget: ESP32 peaks ~500 mA reflected through the LDO, WS2812B up to 60 mA, CH340C ~15 mA). AMS1117-3.3 LDO gives 3V3 at up to 800 mA; dissipation at 500 mA peak is (5−3.3)×0.5 ≈ 0.85 W transient, acceptable on SOT-223 with a copper pour. 10 µF on LDO in/out, 10 µF + 100 nF at the module, 100 nF per sensor supply pin. USB-C sink: two 5.1 k CC pull-downs, USBLC6-2SC6 TVS on D+/D−/VBUS, shield to GND.

**MCU & I/O map:**
- IO21 = SDA, IO22 = SCL, 4.7 k pull-ups to 3V3 (bus is short; ~100 kHz default).
- IO4 → WS2812B DIN (5 V LED driven with 3.3 V logic — works in practice at short trace lengths; note included).
- IO0 → BOOT button, EN → RESET button, 10 k pull-up + 100 nF on EN.
- IO1/IO3 ↔ CH340C RXD/TXD. CH340C RTS → EN via 100 nF for auto-reset; DTR left unconnected (hold BOOT when flashing if auto-boot isn't picked up).
- Avoided IO12 (boot strap), IO6–11 (flash), input-only pins unused.

**Peripherals:** SHT31: ADDR, R → GND; nRESET → 3V3. BME280 in I2C mode: CSB → VDDIO, SDO → GND. Both sensors placed away from the LDO/ESP32 heat (placement note). Qwiic connector carries GND/3V3/SDA/SCL.

**Indication:** Green power LED on 3V3 via 1 k (~1.5 mA). WS2812B with its own 100 nF.

**Trade-offs:** AMS1117 chosen over AP2112K for current headroom during Wi-Fi bursts; CH340C rather than an ESP32-S3 keeps the BOM familiar and cheap. 31 parts, 2-layer, ~56×40 mm.
