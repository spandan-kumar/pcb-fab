## Reasoning

**Requirements → architecture.** A classic Arduino-Nano-class board: ATmega328P-AU running at 5 V / 16 MHz, programmed over USB-C through a CH340C with DTR auto-reset, plus ICSP for bootloader burning. Single MCU, no wireless, so a 2-layer board is easy.

**Power tree.** USB-C VBUS (5 V, budget 500 mA) is the main rail; it feeds the ATmega (≈15 mA active), the CH340C (≈12 mA) and the AMS1117-3.3 LDO. The 3V3 rail is for user shields only, budgeted at 300 mA (dissipation (5−3.3)×0.3 ≈ 0.5 W, fine for SOT-223 with a copper pour). 10 µF bulk on both rails, 100 nF on each of the three ATmega supply pins (VCC×2, AVCC). USBLC6-2SC6 TVS clamps D+/D−/VBUS; two independent 5.1 k CC pull-downs advertise a UFP sink.

**MCU & I/O map.** 16 MHz HC-49S crystal on XTAL1/XTAL2 with 2×22 pF. RESET has a 10 k pull-up, a tactile button, and the CH340 DTR line couples through 100 nF to pulse RESET low when avrdude opens the port (standard Arduino auto-reset). UART: CH340 TXD→PD0/RX, CH340 RXD←PD1/TX. TX/RX LEDs hang off those lines through 1 k to 5 V (≈3 mA, light when the line goes low, exactly like the Nano). Power LED: 1 k from 5 V.

**Headers.** J3 (1×10) carries D0–D9 in order; J4 (1×8) carries D10–D13 (SPI) and A0–A3. The brief only named those two headers, but A4/A5 (I²C), RESET and the rails would otherwise be stranded, so a small 1×6 J5 exposes SDA, SCL, 3V3, 5V, GND, RESET — flagged in notes. ICSP 2×3 uses the standard MISO/VCC/SCK/MOSI/RESET/GND pinout.

**Trade-offs.** No VBUS fuse (USB host already current-limits; adding one costs one net class exception). AREF left floating (internal reference default) to save parts; ADC6/ADC7 not broken out since the brief's header count doesn't allow it. 30 components total, ~62×42 mm — Nano-sized but with proper USB-C, TVS and a full 3.3 V rail.
